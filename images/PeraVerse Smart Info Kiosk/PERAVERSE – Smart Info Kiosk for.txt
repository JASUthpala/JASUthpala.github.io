# PERAVERSE – Smart Info Kiosk for EngEx 2025
### Making Exhibition Experiences Smarter and More Interactive

**CO227 – Computer Engineering Project | Department of Computer Engineering | University of Peradeniya | 2025**

---

## 1. Project Overview

PERAVERSE is a real-time digital twin platform developed to enhance visitor experience and crowd management at EngEx 2025, the Faculty of Engineering's annual exhibition.

As part of a team of nine members, we developed the **Smart Info Kiosk**, an interactive, touch-friendly web application designed to help visitors explore the exhibition, discover exhibits, navigate exhibition locations, and access event information conveniently, even without an internet connection.

The platform brings essential exhibition information into a centralized system by integrating interactive navigation, event management, exhibit information, live crowd monitoring, real-time notifications, and an AI-powered conversational assistant.

Designed with accessibility and ease of use in mind, the kiosk provides an intuitive interface suitable for visitors of different ages and technical backgrounds.

## 2. Motivation

University exhibitions attract large numbers of visitors who need to access information about events, exhibits, departments, demonstrations, and exhibition locations.

However, finding relevant information in a large exhibition environment can be challenging. Visitors may have difficulty locating specific exhibits, identifying ongoing events, understanding the exhibition layout, or obtaining answers to general questions.

Traditional information boards and printed guides provide limited interactivity and may not always offer up-to-date information.

These challenges motivated us to develop PERAVERSE, a centralized digital platform that makes exhibition information more accessible while improving visitor engagement and supporting exhibition management.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Managing visitor information and navigation during a large-scale exhibition presents several challenges.

- **Information Accessibility:** Visitors may struggle to find relevant information about exhibits, events, departments, and demonstrations.
- **Navigation Difficulties:** Locating specific buildings and exhibition areas can be difficult, particularly for first-time visitors.
- **Crowd Management:** Understanding visitor distribution across different buildings and exhibition areas is important for effective crowd management.
- **Event Awareness:** Visitors may miss important sessions, demonstrations, or scheduled events without timely notifications.
- **Limited Interaction:** Traditional information boards and printed guides do not provide conversational or personalized assistance.
- **Information Centralization:** Exhibition information is often distributed across different sources, making it difficult for visitors to access everything in one place.

The main challenge was to create an accessible, interactive, and centralized information system that could improve the overall exhibition experience.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

PERAVERSE introduces an interactive Smart Info Kiosk that combines multiple exhibition-related services into one web-based platform.

The system provides visitors with a centralized interface to explore exhibition information, navigate locations, monitor event schedules, receive notifications, and interact with an AI-powered chatbot.

The platform also supports exhibition management through features such as crowd monitoring, event updates, announcements, and visitor feedback collection.

**Main objectives:**

- Provide centralized access to exhibition information.
- Improve visitor navigation through interactive maps.
- Display event schedules and real-time event statuses.
- Provide information about exhibits and departments.
- Support crowd monitoring through visual representations.
- Deliver timely notifications and announcements.
- Enable conversational interaction through an AI-powered chatbot.
- Collect visitor feedback to support exhibition evaluation.
- Provide an accessible kiosk experience, including offline usage.

## 5. Key Features

### 5.1 Event Management

The platform provides a searchable event schedule that allows visitors to explore exhibition activities.

Key functionalities include:

- Searchable event listings.
- Ongoing, Upcoming, and Completed event statuses.
- Event ratings.
- Automatic notifications.
- Live countdowns for upcoming events.

### 5.2 Exhibit Information

Visitors can explore different exhibition exhibits through a centralized information interface.

The system provides:

- Exhibit descriptions.
- Search functionality.
- Relevant tags.
- Location details.
- Information to help visitors discover exhibits of interest.

### 5.3 Interactive Exhibition Map

The interactive map helps visitors locate exhibition buildings and exhibits.

Key functionalities include:

- Building search.
- Location-based navigation.
- Highlighting selected buildings and exhibits.
- Linking relevant information to exhibition locations.

### 5.4 Live Crowd Monitoring

The platform includes visual crowd monitoring to help represent visitor distribution across exhibition buildings.

Key functionalities include:

- Colour-coded crowd density heat maps.
- Visitor count visualization.
- Building capacity information.
- Visual representation of crowd distribution.

### 5.5 Real-Time Notifications

The platform supports automatic event alerts and custom announcements.

An administrative interface allows announcements to be managed and delivered to visitors through the kiosk.

### 5.6 AI-Powered Chatbot

An integrated conversational assistant helps visitors quickly find exhibition-related information.

Visitors can ask questions about events, exhibits, departments, demonstrations, and general exhibition information.

The chatbot also supports location-related responses that connect visitors to the interactive exhibition map.

### 5.7 Visitor Feedback

The system provides a feedback mechanism through which visitors can submit ratings and optional comments.

Feedback is stored in PostgreSQL, with automatic CSV backups to support data preservation and further analysis.

## 6. System Architecture

PERAVERSE integrates multiple software components to provide a centralized exhibition information platform.

### 6.1 Frontend Application

The frontend provides the interactive user interface through which visitors access the kiosk functionalities.

It includes the event management interface, exhibit information pages, interactive map, crowd monitoring views, notifications, and chatbot interface.

### 6.2 Backend Services

The backend handles application requests, processes information, and provides REST APIs for communication between the frontend and database.

It also supports chatbot query processing and other application functionalities.

### 6.3 Database Layer

The database stores structured exhibition information and visitor-related data.

Supabase and PostgreSQL were used to support structured data management and database queries.

### 6.4 AI Chatbot Component

The chatbot operates as a conversational interface integrated into the main kiosk application.

It receives visitor questions, processes queries, searches relevant exhibition information, and generates responses.

Location-related responses are also connected to the interactive exhibition map.

## 7. My Contribution – AI Chatbot Development

One of my primary responsibilities in PERAVERSE was developing the AI-powered chatbot together with Deshan.

The chatbot was designed to serve as a conversational assistant within the Smart Info Kiosk, allowing visitors to access exhibition information through natural-language questions rather than manually searching through different sections of the application.

### 7.1 Chatbot Functionalities

The chatbot was designed to answer visitor questions related to:

- Event schedules and session details.
- Exhibit locations and descriptions.
- Departmental information.
- Ongoing demonstrations and talks.
- General FAQs about EngEx 2025.

It also supported location-based responses that connected visitors with the interactive exhibition map.

### 7.2 Frontend Development

I contributed to developing the chatbot frontend using React.js.

The frontend included:

- A dedicated chatbot page.
- Interactive chat interface.
- Message display and conversation interaction.
- An easily accessible chatbot icon across kiosk screens.
- Integration with the backend chatbot API.

The interface was designed to make the chatbot easily accessible without interrupting the visitor's navigation through the kiosk.

### 7.3 Backend Development

I contributed to developing the chatbot backend using Node.js and Express.js.

The backend REST API handled chatbot requests, processed visitor queries, managed chat sessions, and generated responses.

The implementation included:

- API endpoint development.
- Query processing.
- Response generation.
- Session tracking.
- Error handling.
- Communication between the frontend and database.

### 7.4 Database Integration

Supabase and PostgreSQL were integrated to support structured exhibition information and knowledge-base queries.

The chatbot used stored information to retrieve relevant details and provide answers to visitor questions.

### 7.5 Query Processing and Response Optimization

Several mechanisms were implemented to improve chatbot functionality and reliability.

These included:

- **Keyword Extraction:** Identifying important terms from visitor questions.
- **Case-Insensitive Search:** Improving query matching regardless of letter capitalization.
- **Response Caching:** Reusing previously generated responses where appropriate to reduce repeated processing.
- **Rate Limiting:** Controlling request frequency to protect the backend from excessive requests.
- **Session Tracking:** Maintaining information about individual chat sessions.
- **Error Handling:** Managing unexpected situations and improving system stability.

### 7.6 Interactive Map Integration

An important aspect of the chatbot was its integration with the interactive exhibition map.

When visitors asked location-related questions, the chatbot could provide relevant location information linked to the map.

This allowed visitors to move from conversational information retrieval to visual navigation within the exhibition.

## 10. Challenges and Solutions

Developing the chatbot involved several challenges related to query processing, information retrieval, system integration, and performance.

### 10.1 Understanding Visitor Queries

**Challenge:** Visitors may ask the same question using different words, sentence structures, or capitalization.

**Approach:** Keyword extraction and case-insensitive search were implemented to improve query matching.

### 10.2 Retrieving Relevant Exhibition Information

**Challenge:** The chatbot needed to provide relevant information from structured exhibition data.

**Approach:** Supabase and PostgreSQL were integrated to support structured knowledge-base queries.

### 10.3 Frontend and Backend Integration

**Challenge:** The chatbot interface needed to communicate reliably with the backend services.

**Approach:** REST APIs were implemented using Node.js and Express.js to handle chatbot requests and responses.

### 10.4 Response Performance

**Challenge:** Repeated queries and excessive requests could affect backend performance.

**Approach:** Response caching and rate limiting were introduced to improve efficiency and control request frequency.

### 10.5 Maintaining Conversation Sessions

**Challenge:** Chat interactions needed to be organized and associated with individual sessions.

**Approach:** Session tracking was implemented to support chatbot interaction management.
