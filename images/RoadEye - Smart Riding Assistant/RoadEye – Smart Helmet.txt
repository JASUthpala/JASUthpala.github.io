# RoadEye – Smart Helmet
### Smarter Riding. Safer Journeys.

**RoadEye is an integrated smart motorcycle safety system designed to improve rider awareness, minimize distractions, and enhance road safety through intelligent hardware, software, and real-time communication.**

## 1. Project Overview

RoadEye is a smart motorcycle safety system developed to enhance rider awareness and reduce accident risks. It integrates three main components: a smart helmet, a bike-mounted module, and a companion mobile application.

By combining sensor technologies, wireless communication, a heads-up display (HUD), and intelligent software features, RoadEye provides riders with essential information and safety alerts without requiring them to take their hands off the handlebars or frequently look away from the road.

The system is designed around hands-free and eyes-free interaction, allowing riders to access navigation information, riding data, and important notifications through visual, audio, and haptic feedback.

## 2. Motivation

Motorcycle riders are particularly vulnerable on the road because they have limited protection and depend heavily on their own awareness and ability to respond to changing road conditions.

Unlike modern cars equipped with advanced driver-assistance systems, most motorcycles lack integrated technologies that provide intelligent navigation assistance, environmental awareness, and emergency support.

Furthermore, riders often need to look at their mobile phones for navigation, manually check their surroundings, or interact with devices while riding. These activities can increase distraction and cognitive load.

These challenges motivated us to develop RoadEye: a smarter riding solution that brings essential information directly to the rider while maintaining focus on the road.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Existing motorcycle safety solutions have several limitations:

- **Navigation Distractions:** Riders often need to look at mobile phone screens to follow navigation instructions.
- **Limited Situational Awareness:** Conventional mirrors and helmets may not provide sufficient awareness of surrounding conditions and potential hazards.
- **Delayed Emergency Response:** After an accident, contacting emergency contacts and communicating the rider's situation may be difficult.
- **Lack of Integrated Safety Features:** Navigation, riding information, environmental monitoring, and emergency support are generally provided through separate devices or applications.
- **Manual Interaction:** Many existing solutions require riders to interact physically with devices, increasing distraction.

The main challenge is to provide essential riding information and safety assistance without introducing additional distractions or requiring unnecessary manual interaction.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

RoadEye introduces an integrated smart helmet system supported by a bike-mounted module and a mobile application.

The helmet delivers important information through a heads-up display, audio output, and haptic feedback. The bike module collects and communicates relevant riding and sensor information, while the mobile application provides navigation, system configuration, notifications, and emergency-related functionality.

The system aims to:

- Provide navigation information through a helmet-mounted display.
- Improve rider awareness through sensor-based information.
- Deliver notifications using visual, audio, and haptic feedback.
- Support emergency communication.
- Provide weather and other relevant riding information.
- Enable wireless communication between the helmet, bike module, and mobile application.
- Offer a more convenient and less distracting riding experience.

## 5. System Architecture

RoadEye consists of three major components that work together to provide an integrated riding experience.

### 5.1 Smart Helmet Unit

The smart helmet is the primary user-interaction component. It is designed to deliver important information directly to the rider.

**Main components and functionalities:**

- Heads-Up Display (HUD) for navigation and other important information.
- Audio output through an integrated speaker unit.
- Haptic feedback for alerts and notifications.
- LED indicators for visual status information.
- Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) for motion and orientation-related data.
- Helmet buckle and wearing-related sensing.
- Wireless communication with the bike module and mobile application.

### 5.2 Bike-Mounted Module

The bike module acts as an additional sensing and communication unit.

It supports the collection and transmission of relevant riding information and enables communication between the motorcycle-related hardware and the rest of the RoadEye system.

**Main responsibilities:**

- Collecting sensor information.
- Communicating with the helmet and mobile application.
- Supporting the distribution of relevant riding information.
- Managing power-related hardware components.
- Supporting the overall system's wireless communication.

### 5.3 Mobile Application

The mobile application acts as the main software interface for RoadEye.

It allows users to access navigation, manage system settings, view information, and use safety-related features.

**Main features:**

- User registration and authentication.
- Dashboard with relevant riding information.
- Navigation and destination selection.
- Weather information.
- Emergency contact management.
- Notifications and system status.
- Music service integration.
- Ride-related information and summaries.

## 6. Key Features

### 6.1 Heads-Up Display Navigation

The HUD presents navigation information within the rider's field of view, reducing the need to look down at a mobile phone.

The navigation interface was developed to display essential information in a compact format suitable for the helmet visor.

### 6.2 Wireless Communication

RoadEye uses wireless communication to exchange information between the mobile application, helmet, and bike module.

The communication system was developed to transfer sensor information and navigation-related content while maintaining coordination between the components.

### 6.3 Emergency Assistance

The mobile application includes an emergency section that allows users to select up to five emergency contacts.

The application can use the selected contacts to support emergency message communication.

### 6.4 Weather Information

The application provides weather information relevant to the rider's current location and navigation destination.

Weather information is presented through the application dashboard using visual indicators.

### 6.5 Audio and Music Integration

The application supports access to external music services, allowing users to select supported services and launch the corresponding applications.

Song-related information can also be shared with the helmet display.

### 6.6 Sensor Integration

RoadEye integrates sensor modules to collect relevant information about movement and riding conditions.

The collected data can be transmitted to the application and backend for processing and presentation.

### 6.7 Additional Safety and Convenience Features

The overall design also considers features such as:

- Helmet buckle reminders.
- Helmet wearing detection.
- Anti-theft functionality.
- Riding information summaries.

## 9. Technical Challenges and Solutions

Developing RoadEye involved several challenges across hardware design, software development, and system integration.

### 9.1 Helmet Display Integration

**Challenge:** Integrating a navigation display into a motorcycle helmet while maintaining visibility and usability.

**Approach:** We experimented with the HUD projection concept, refined the display interface, and improved the visor navigation layout based on testing and panel feedback.

### 9.2 Wireless Data Communication

**Challenge:** Establishing reliable communication between the mobile application and ESP32 module.

**Approach:** We implemented Wi-Fi communication using UDP packets and binary data transmission, resolving connectivity and data-transfer issues during integration.

### 9.3 Navigation Frame Transmission

**Challenge:** Transmitting navigation information to the helmet display while minimizing unnecessary data transfer and delays.

**Approach:** We explored JPEG image-frame transmission and the transfer of changed frames to improve the efficiency of the communication process.

### 9.4 Audio Transmission

**Challenge:** Achieving stable audio transmission through the helmet speaker.

**Approach:** We tested the audio functionality during system integration and continued working on resolving the initial sound-quality and continuous-transmission issues.

### 9.5 Hardware Design and Safety

**Challenge:** Designing a helmet enclosure that accommodates the electronic components while considering weight distribution, comfort, and rider safety.

**Approach:** We redesigned the hardware enclosure and developed an improved mouthpiece after identifying a potential safety issue.

### 9.6 Power Management

**Challenge:** Ensuring that the helmet and bike modules could operate for a practical duration.

**Approach:** We developed the power circuit and conducted battery tests under continuous operation to evaluate operating time.

### 9.7 Software and Hardware Integration

**Challenge:** Combining the mobile application, backend, ESP32, and helmet hardware into one coordinated system.

**Approach:** We developed and tested the individual components before progressively integrating them, identifying and resolving issues during each stage.
