Industrial Visit to OREL IT & Institute of Bankers of Sri Lanka (IBSL)
Organized by: SLASSCOM in collaboration with the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya.

I had the opportunity to participate in an industrial visit to OREL IT and the Institute of Bankers of Sri Lanka (IBSL), gaining valuable exposure to professional work environments, industry practices, and career opportunities in the technology sector.

 

Key Highlights & Experiences
1. Career Readiness Session – IBSL

Participated in an informative session conducted by Mr. Yasoma Jayasinghe, focusing on:

Internship opportunities and career preparation.

Interview techniques and professional presentation.

Building a strong professional image.

Understanding industry expectations and standards.

2. Personal & Professional Development

Attended an interactive session with Mr. Udayanga Weerarathne, gaining valuable insights into:

Personal growth and self-confidence.

Effective communication skills.

Leadership and professional development.

Planning and preparing for future career opportunities.

3. Teamwork & Networking

Collaborated with students from Uva Wellassa University, providing an opportunity to exchange ideas, build professional connections, and strengthen teamwork, communication, and leadership skills.

4. Industrial Exposure – OREL IT

Visited OREL IT and explored different departments, gaining insights into:

Real-world industrial workflows and practices.

Technologies used in the IT industry.

Organizational processes and ongoing projects.

Career opportunities and professional pathways in the technology sector.

 
Key Takeaways
This experience helped me bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industrial applications while broadening my understanding of professional environments. It also strengthened my communication, teamwork, networking, and career-readiness skills as a future Computer Engineer.

Acknowledgement: Special thanks to SLASSCOM, the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya, the participating organizations, and all the speakers and organizers for providing this valuable learning opportunity.
