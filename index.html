INDUSTRIAL VISIT

Industrial Visit to OREL IT & Institute of Bankers of Sri Lanka (IBSL)

Organized by SLASSCOM in collaboration with the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya. The visit included career-readiness and professional-development sessions at IBSL, followed by exposure to teams, workflows and opportunities at OREL IT.

Meeting students from Uva Wellassa University and hearing from industry professionals offered valuable perspectives on communication, teamwork and preparing for a technology career.

  • At IBSL, Mr. Yasoma Jayasinghe led a career-readiness session covering internships, interview preparation, and industry expectations.
  • A personal-development session with Mr. Udayanga Weerarathne focused on confidence, communication, and leadership.
  • At OREL IT, we learned about teams, workflows, technologies, and career opportunities in the IT industry.
