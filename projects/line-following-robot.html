GP 118 · MINI PROJECT

Line Following Robot

A first hands-on robotics project using an Arduino, IR sensors, DC motors, and a custom circuit to autonomously follow a predefined path.

ArduinoMicrocontrollers IR SensorsEmbedded Systems

Project overview

Developed for GP 118 at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya, this mini project introduced our team to practical embedded systems. The robot uses IR sensors to detect a marked track and control its DC motors to follow the path. Building it provided experience with sensor integration, motor control, circuitry, teamwork, and problem-solving.
