PERAVERSE — Smart Info Kiosk

A touch-friendly exhibition information kiosk built to help EngEx 2025 visitors discover exhibits, navigate the venue, and access event information.

Full-stack DevelopmentWeb Applications AI ChatbotDatabase Systems

Project overview

PERAVERSE was developed by a team of nine for CO227 at the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya. The Smart Info Kiosk brings exhibition navigation, event and exhibit information, crowd updates, notifications, and a conversational assistant into one visitor-facing platform. The kiosk was designed to remain useful even when an internet connection is unavailable.

My contribution

My contribution focused on developing the AI chatbot, helping visitors ask questions about the exhibition and find relevant information through a conversational interface.

Platform features

  • Searchable events, event statuses, and visitor notifications.
  • Exhibit descriptions, tags, and location information.
  • An interactive map for finding exhibition buildings and exhibits.
  • Visual crowd distribution and building capacity information.
  • AI chatbot answers linked to exhibition and location information.
  • Visitor feedback and support for offline kiosk usage.
