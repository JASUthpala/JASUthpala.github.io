Project overview

As part of Team EurekaWave at the University of Peradeniya, we developed a concept for an intelligent early-warning and deterrence system addressing the risks of human-elephant encounters in Sri Lanka. The team was selected among the Top 8 finalists of the SLT Technnovation Challenge 2025.

Our Project: Smart Eco-Guard System

Our solution focuses on reducing conflict between rural communities and wildlife with a smart early-warning and deterrence system designed for jungle environments. It brings together acoustic and visual detection, local processing, non-harmful deterrents, and wireless alerts.

  • Acoustic detection: Directional, high-sensitivity microphones are designed to detect low-frequency rumbles and trumpeting as early indicators of elephant activity. Real-time sound processing can support faster warnings and response.
  • AI-powered visual detection: Infrared CCTV and motion-sensitive cameras monitor high-risk zones. Footage is intended to be analyzed using a custom-trained YOLO (You Only Look Once) model to identify elephants, including in poor visibility.
  • Edge computing: Local analysis is designed to provide rapid detection without relying on an internet connection.
  • Non-harmful deterrence: When activity is detected, the proposed system can activate bee-sound emitters and floodlights to deter elephants without physical harm.
  • Sustainable operation: Solar power is intended to support off-grid use, while eco-conscious materials help the system integrate with its surroundings.
  • Community alerts: Wi-Fi-enabled notifications are designed to inform villagers and wildlife officials in real-time.

The goal is to support earlier awareness and safer coexistence between rural communities and wildlife, while avoiding harmful deterrence and reducing dependence on grid power and internet access.
