Human–Elephant Conflict Mitigation

A Smart Eco-Guard concept that combines acoustic and visual sensing to support early warnings and non-harmful responses near communities.

AIComputer VisionIoT Embedded SystemsEdge Computing

Project overview

As part of Team EurekaWave at the University of Peradeniya, we developed a concept for an intelligent early-warning and deterrence system addressing the risks of human-elephant encounters in Sri Lanka. The team was selected among the Top 8 finalists of the SLT Technnovation Challenge 2025.

Problem and proposed approach

Manual monitoring and reactive responses can be difficult in remote or low-visibility areas. The concept combines directional acoustic sensing with infrared cameras and motion sensors. A local processing unit is proposed to analyze sensor information, with non-harmful deterrence and wireless alerts considered as possible responses when activity is detected.

Solar power and edge computing are part of the design so that a deployed system could operate in locations with limited access to electricity or reliable internet.
