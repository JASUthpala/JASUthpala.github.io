MRI Motion Artifact Reduction

Exploring GAN-based approaches to reduce rigid motion artifacts in abdominal MRI while preserving meaningful structural details.

PythonGANsPyTorch / DL Medical Imaging

Research overview

This research explores deep-learning methods for reducing rigid motion artifacts in abdominal MRI. The aim is to investigate image restoration while retaining anatomical structures relevant to interpretation.

Research problem and objective

Patient motion can introduce artifacts that affect the appearance of MRI images. The research objective is to explore whether a generative adversarial network can reduce these artifacts while preserving the underlying structural details.

Approach and evaluation
