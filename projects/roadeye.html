RoadEye — Smart Riding Assistant

An integrated smart motorcycle safety system combining a smart helmet, a bike-mounted module, and a companion mobile application to support rider awareness with hands-free information and alerts.

React NativeSpring BootESP32 PostgreSQLIoT

Project overview

Developed as a third-year group project at the Department of Computer Engineering, University of Peradeniya, RoadEye explores ways to reduce navigation distraction and bring useful riding information to the rider. The helmet provides visual, audio, and haptic feedback; the mobile app supports navigation and system interaction; and the bike-mounted module collects and communicates relevant sensor information.

System components and features

Looking down at a phone or manually interacting with devices can distract a rider. RoadEye brings key information together through connected system components:

  • Smart helmet: A heads-up display, audio output, haptic feedback, and LED indicators present navigation and alerts. The design also includes motion sensing and helmet-wearing detection.
  • Bike-mounted module: Collects relevant sensor information and supports communication between the motorcycle hardware, helmet, and app.
  • Mobile app: Supports navigation, weather, notifications, system settings, ride information, and emergency contact management. The project notes describe support for up to five emergency contacts.

The project was developed by Group 15. My role was as a project team member.
